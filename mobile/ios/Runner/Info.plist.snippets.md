# iOS Info.plist additions

Add to `ios/Runner/Info.plist` (generated by `flutter create` first):

```xml
<!-- WhatsApp deep-link allowed scheme -->
<key>LSApplicationQueriesSchemes</key>
<array>
    <string>whatsapp</string>
    <string>https</string>
</array>

<!-- Forbid plaintext HTTP -->
<key>NSAppTransportSecurity</key>
<dict>
    <key>NSAllowsArbitraryLoads</key>
    <false/>
</dict>

<!-- We never use the camera/mic/location/contacts; no permission strings
     are exposed by the binary. Inclusion of those frameworks is a finding
     in App Store review for messaging apps. -->
```

Keychain entitlement (already implicit; keep `kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`).

To suppress iOS app preview in the background (recents view), do not rely on
`flutter_windowmanager` (Android-only). The `screen_protector` package's
`protectDataLeakageWithBlur()` overlays a UIVisualEffectView when the app
resigns active.
